# capstone-project
Habit-tracking-web-app

This webapp is desined to encourage users to keep up with their habits and not just do them once. The purpose is to excite people with the idea that they can get their habbits complete and be able to interact with virtual pets as a result. The main features are the ability to add, edit and delete habbits, get stars for completing the habbits and using these stars to unlock pets. There will be a shop of pets where they can spend their stars on any pet of their choosing. That pet will be there to cheer them on as they continue their habbits.

The process of making the application required the help of react, html/css/javascript, node, SQL and prisma.
